## 1. Overview
Middle third of a Vox-style "history of Porsche" explainer covering the 911 launch (1964), the 1960s US success, and the model line-up 914 → 924 → 928 → 944 → Boxster (1996) up to a 1998–2005 sales bar chart.
Visual identity: **one accent colour only — a hot lemon highlighter yellow (#F7FA0A)** — used for highlighter boxes, multiply discs, brush strokes, offset outlines, glow, chart gridlines and caption text; everything else is archival material (B&W / faded colour photos, newspaper clippings, brochure pages, film footage) placed on **warm off-white paper with a faint graph grid and a strong grey vignette**, set in **editorial serif type** (regular serif titles on yellow boxes, bold-italic serif yellow captions with a black ▶ bullet). Graphics animate in **12 fps steps (2-3-2-3 frame pattern in 30 fps)** with snappy ease-out entrances, while held graphics "breathe" with a 6 fps sub-pixel jitter. Scenes change by hard cuts disguised as **zoom-throughs, slide-swaps and "photo drop" swaps** (49 cuts in 66 s; median scene well under 2 s).

## 6. Motion
### Cadence
- Range gap histogram (frames between changes): {1: 556, 2: 251, 3: 175, 4: 12, 5: 41, 8: 6, 10: 4, …}. Graphic animation (discs, wipes, slides, camera moves on stills, magnifier, chart bars, montage dissolves/push) steps on the **2-3-2-3 pattern = 12 fps**. Live footage, newspaper push, map zoom, TV scene and doodles, framed-footage slide run on **ones**.
- **Hold jitter ("breathing")**: once a graphic scene stops animating, the whole graphic layer (car + title, card, map…) re-jitters **every 5 frames (6 fps)** by 0–1 px@360 (0–3 px@1080) and scale ±0.2–0.5%, while the paper stays fixed (f2611/2616/2621/2626/2631; 2546/2551/2556/2561/2566; 2381/2386/2391; 3732/3737/3742/3747). ## 11. Uncertainties
- **Exact yellows**: thin text, 1–2 px outlines and JPEG 4:2:0 chroma subsampling shift the yellow toward lime (#D5E743–#E7F181). The opaque boxes (#F4F90D–#F9FF08) are the most reliable reading; I assume one master yellow (#F7FA0A) for all uses, but the caption text might be a separate, slightly greener yellow.
- **Typefaces** are visual matches only (Georgia/Gelasio italics for captions and labels; Tiempos-/Newsreader-like serif for titles and chart). Not identifiable with certainty at 360p.
- **Multiply vs. normal**: discs, print highlights and map brush clearly multiply (blend fit on f2035/f2060). Title/label boxes are uniform and opaque (could be normal blend or multiply over white paper — indistinguishable).
- Easing names are fits to 3–12 sample points stepped at 12 fps; treat as "ease-out"/"ease-in-out" families rather than exact curves.
- Matte texture of the montage dissolves could not be extracted (only coverage vs time); the 3.25 px 45° halftone seen in one montage photo is probably inherent to that scan.
- Grain: none measurable at 360p; a fine grain at 1080p may have been destroyed by compression.
- The 6 fps "hold jitter" amplitude (0–1 px@360) is near the measurement floor; it could be a wiggle expression on the comp camera rather than per-layer.
- The year card shadow and TV RGB split might be baked into the source assets rather than added in the comp.
- Bar heights do not match the printed values exactly (bar 1 ≈ 40.7k by geometry vs label 43,982) — suggests hand-placed bars; the chart animation continues into the next agent's range (f3973+).
- Boundary notes: the f1997–2029 caption started before my range (prev agent); the bar chart build continues after f3972 (next agent).
